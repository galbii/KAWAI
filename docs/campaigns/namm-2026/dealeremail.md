## Email Strategy Notes

### Key Messaging Priorities:
1. **100th Anniversary Celebration** - Creates emotional connection and sense of occasion
2. **Special NAMM Pricing** - Available throughout the show (primary financial incentive)
3. **Dealer Reception** - Intimate networking opportunity with leadership
4. **Both Events Working Together** - Booth for products, reception for relationships

### Subtle Networking Encouragement:
- "Connect & Network" positioned as a core benefit of the reception
- "Build relationships with fellow dealers" language throughout
- Live jazz performance (David Arnay Trio) creates relaxed atmosphere for conversation
- Warm, welcoming tone invites natural interaction
- Mr. Kawai's presence encourages dealers to attend and connect

### Design Strategy:
- **Clean & Minimalistic** - Modern, Apple-inspired aesthetic with ample white space
- **Professional Imagery** - "Instrumental to Life" logo (dark gray header #2C2C2C) and NAMM 2026 badge imagery
- **Real Photos** - Each exclusive event item features actual photography instead of icons/emojis
- **Alternating Layout** - Image/text layout alternates left-right for visual rhythm (50/50 split)
- **Uniform Image Sizes** - All images are 50% width for consistency and balance
- **Premium Background** - Crystal piano background image behind Special NAMM Pricing (no overlay, enhanced text shadows for readability)
- **Mobile responsive** - Optimized for on-the-go viewing with collapsible sections
- **Clear hierarchy**: Hero → Badge → Exclusive Event (4 items with photos) → NAMM Pricing → Two Events → CTA
- **Visual simplicity**: Simple dividers, real images, clean typography
- **Emotional appeal**: 100th anniversary badge image creates significance without overwhelming
- **Single CTA Strategy**: "Learn More" action throughout - no pressure, informative approach
- **Photography-Driven**: Each event element showcased with professional photography

### Exclusive Event Highlights (4 Key Items with Photography):
1. **Catering** (Image: Food/dining photo) - Hors d'oeuvres and drinks as appreciation for partnership
2. **Connection** (Image: People networking) - Meet fellow dealers, create connections, expand network
3. **Kentaro Kawai** (Image: President portrait) - Meet the president and hear vision for the future
4. **Live Music** (Image: Jazz performance) - David Arnay Trio - LA's finest jazz ensemble, long-time Kawai artist

### Testing Recommendations:
- **A/B test subject lines** focusing on pricing vs. anniversary vs. exclusive reception
- **Track click rates** for booth vs. reception CTAs
- **Monitor RSVP conversion** from initial send vs. reminders
- **Test send times** - Tuesday/Wednesday mid-morning typically performs best for B2B
