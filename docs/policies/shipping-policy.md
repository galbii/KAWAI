# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** April 2, 2026  
**Applies To:** All purchases made directly at kawai.com  
**Delivery Area:** Contiguous United States (48 states)

---

## How We Deliver

Every Kawai instrument deserves to arrive the way it was built — with care. We don't drop a $5,000 piano on your doorstep in a box. Our delivery tiers are designed around the instrument, not just the zip code.

---

## Digital Pianos (CN, ES, KDP Series)

**Standard Home Delivery — Free on all orders**

- Threshold delivery: delivered to your front door or first dry area
- Estimated delivery: **7–14 business days** from order confirmation
- You'll receive a tracking link within 24 hours of shipment
- Email and SMS updates at every major transit milestone

**Want it set up?** Add White-Glove Delivery at checkout for $149:
- Placement in the room of your choice
- Assembly of stand and pedal unit
- Basic orientation (connectivity, initial settings)
- Removal of all packaging materials

---

## Hybrid & Upright Acoustic Pianos

**White-Glove Delivery — Included, no extra charge**

These instruments require professional handling. White-glove delivery is standard for every hybrid and acoustic upright:

- Delivery by a trained 2-person piano moving team
- Placement in the room of your choice, up to 2 flights of stairs at no charge
- Bench placement and lid adjustment
- Removal of all packaging
- Estimated delivery: **14–21 business days** from order confirmation

We use specialized piano freight carriers — not general freight. Your instrument is loaded by people who handle pianos every day.

---

## Grand Pianos (GL, GX, Shigeru Kawai SK Series)

**Concierge Delivery — Included, fully managed**

Grand piano delivery is a dedicated service, not a shipping event.

What's included:
- Pre-delivery site assessment call — we confirm doorway widths, staircase access, room dimensions, and floor type before scheduling
- Delivery by certified piano technicians and movers
- Placement, leg installation, lid rod assembly, and bench positioning
- First-tuning scheduled within 60–90 days of delivery (at no charge — this is part of your purchase, not an upsell)
- Post-delivery check-in call from your Kawai customer relationship manager

Estimated delivery: **3–6 weeks** from order confirmation, depending on location and model availability. For in-stock models, we can often do better — we'll give you a firm window during order confirmation.

---

## Delivery Windows & Scheduling

We do not do "surprise" deliveries. Every shipment is scheduled with you in advance:

- For digital pianos: you'll receive a delivery window (AM or PM) the day before
- For hybrid, upright, and grand: you'll schedule a specific date and 2-hour window
- Reschedules are free and easy — just call or email 24 hours before your scheduled delivery

---

## Tracking

All orders receive a tracking link via email and SMS as soon as the instrument ships. For white-glove and concierge deliveries, your delivery team will call or text the morning of delivery with an estimated arrival time.

---

## Delivery Exceptions

**Hawaii & Alaska:** Available for digital pianos only. Additional freight charges apply and will be quoted at checkout. Grand and acoustic pianos are not currently available for direct delivery to HI or AK — please contact us for dealer options.

**International:** We do not currently ship internationally from kawai.com. Please visit kawaiglobal.com for international purchasing options.

**P.O. Boxes:** Not available for any piano shipment.

---

## Damage in Transit

In the unlikely event your piano arrives damaged:

- Note the damage on the delivery receipt before the driver leaves
- Take photos immediately
- Email us at shipping@kawai.com within 48 hours with your order number and photos

We will arrange pickup and either repair or replacement at no cost to you. You will not pay for transit damage under any circumstances.

---

## Order Changes & Cancellations

- Orders may be cancelled for a full refund within **24 hours** of placement
- After 24 hours, if your order has not yet shipped, cancellation is still possible — contact us immediately
- Once an instrument has shipped, our Return Policy applies
