# Best Price Guarantee

**Effective Date:** April 2, 2026  
**Applies To:** All purchases made directly at kawai.com

---

## We Will Match Any Authorized Price

When you buy from kawai.com, you're buying direct from Kawai. No dealer margin. No middleman markup. Our prices reflect what a Kawai instrument should cost — built fairly, priced honestly.

If you find a lower price for the exact same Kawai model from an authorized source, we'll match it. No negotiating. No hoops.

---

## How It Works

**Before you buy:**  
Found a lower price somewhere else? Contact us before checkout. Share the link or screenshot, and we'll match it immediately.

**After you buy:**  
Found a lower price within 14 days of your purchase? Contact us. We'll refund the difference to your original payment method within 5 business days.

---

## What Qualifies

To qualify for a price match, the competing offer must be:

- For the **exact same model** (same finish, same configuration)
- From an **authorized Kawai dealer** — not a gray-market seller, used listing, auction, or unauthorized reseller
- **Currently in stock** and available for purchase (not a clearance price, closeout, or limited-quantity flash sale)
- **Openly advertised** (we don't match prices that require membership, coupon codes, or private negotiation)

---

## What Doesn't Qualify

- eBay, Amazon Marketplace (third-party sellers), Craigslist, or Facebook Marketplace listings
- Prices from sellers who cannot verify factory warranty registration
- Bundle deals where the piano price is obscured by included accessories
- Auction or bid-based pricing
- Prices that have expired or are no longer available when we verify

---

## Why This Matters

A lower price from an unauthorized seller often means:
- **No factory warranty** — Kawai warranty is only valid through authorized sales
- **Gray-market instruments** — potentially imported for a different region, with different voltage specs or missing domestic service support
- **No recourse** — if something goes wrong, you're on your own

Our price match guarantee exists because we're confident our direct pricing is fair — and because we want you to buy with complete confidence, not second-guessing whether you could have done better.

---

## How to Submit a Price Match Request

Email us at pricing@kawai.com with:
1. Your order number (if post-purchase) or the model you're considering
2. A link or screenshot of the competing price
3. The name of the competing seller

We'll respond within 1 business day.
