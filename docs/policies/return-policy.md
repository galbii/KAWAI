## Condition Requirements

To qualify for return under any tier:

- Piano must be in original, undamaged condition with no signs of abuse
- Return must be initiated within the applicable window from delivery date
- Original accessories (bench, power adapter, etc.) should be included if possible — but missing accessories will not disqualify a return; we'll deduct replacement cost only

We do **not** require original packaging for digital pianos. We **do not** charge restocking fees under any circumstances.

---

## What We Don't Cover

- Pianos purchased from unauthorized third-party sellers (eBay, Amazon Marketplace, Craigslist). Those are not our sales and are not covered by this policy.
- Damage caused by accident, misuse, or environmental conditions (flooding, fire, etc.)
- Pianos that have been modified, painted, or structurally altered

## Exchange Program

Changed your mind on the model? No need to return and repurchase. Contact us to initiate a direct exchange:

- Price difference is credited (if upgrading) or refunded (if downgrading)
- We coordinate delivery of the new instrument and pickup of the original in a single visit where possible
- Available within 60 days of original delivery for all tiers
