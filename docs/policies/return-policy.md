# Return Policy

**Effective Date:** April 2, 2026
**Applies To:** Instruments purchased directly from Kawai America at kawaius.com

---

## 15-Day No-Questions-Asked Return Policy

We stand behind every instrument we make. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within **15 days of delivery** — no questions asked.

**Conditions:**
- The instrument must be returned in its original packaging and in new, unplayed condition
- Accessories (bench, adapter, etc.) are not eligible for return
- Original and return shipping charges are **non-refundable**
- You are responsible for the cost of return shipping

---

## Shipping Damage

If your instrument arrives with visible or concealed damage, report it within **5 days of delivery**.

Email **contact@kawaius.com** with your model, serial number, order number, and photos of the damage. When shipping damage is confirmed, return shipping and replacement are covered by Kawai at no cost to you.

---

## Defective Instrument

In the rare event your instrument doesn't perform as expected, contact us as soon as possible.

Email **contact@kawaius.com** or call **(310) 631-1771** (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm PT) with your model, serial number, and order number. A product specialist will assess the issue. If a defect is confirmed, Kawai will arrange repair or replacement — including shipping both ways — at no cost to you.

---

## How to Return

### 1. Email Us
Send an email to **contact@kawaius.com** with your model, serial number, and original order number. Our team will review your request and send return instructions within 1–2 business days.

### 2. Pack Your Piano
Repack the instrument securely in its original box and packaging materials. Once you've shipped it back, we'll handle the rest and send a confirmation when we receive and inspect the return.

### 3. Your Refund
Once the instrument passes inspection, your refund will be issued to your original payment method. Please allow **5–10 business days** for the refund to appear. Your refund reflects the full purchase price **minus original and return shipping costs**.
