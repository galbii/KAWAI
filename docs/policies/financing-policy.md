## Available Plans

### 0% APR Promotional Financing

**Qualifying purchases: $999 and above**

| Purchase Amount | Promotional Period |
|---|---|
| $999 – $2,999 | 12 months, 0% APR |
| $3,000 – $5,999 | 18 months, 0% APR |
| $6,000 – $14,999 | 24 months, 0% APR |
| $15,000 and above | 36 months, 0% APR |

During the promotional period, you pay no interest — just equal monthly payments divided over your term. Pay off early at any time, no penalty.

*If the balance is not paid in full by the end of the promotional period, standard APR applies to the remaining balance. Current standard APR: 19.99% (subject to creditworthiness).*

### Extended Term Financing

For customers who prefer lower monthly payments over a longer period, we offer 48-, 60-, and 72-month plans. Interest rates vary by credit profile and are disclosed clearly before you accept.

## How to Apply

Financing is available at checkout. The process takes about 2 minutes:

1. Select your instrument and proceed to checkout
2. Choose **"Apply for Financing"** as your payment method
3. Complete the short credit application (soft inquiry first — no impact to your credit score until you accept)
4. Receive an instant decision in most cases
5. If approved, select your term and complete your purchase

Applications are handled by [Financing Partner Name]. Your information is transmitted securely and is not shared beyond what's needed for the credit decision.

---

## Who Can Apply

- US residents 18 years of age or older
- Valid US billing address
- Social Security Number or ITIN required for application

Being approved for financing does not affect your eligibility for our Return Guarantee, Price Match Guarantee, or Warranty coverage. All policies apply equally to financed purchases.
