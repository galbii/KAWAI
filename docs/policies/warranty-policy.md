## The Direct Advantage

When you buy from kawai.com, you're buying from Kawai. Your warranty is registered directly with the manufacturer — no dealer intermediary, no gray-area coverage. If something goes wrong, you call us. We own it.

Instruments purchased from unauthorized third-party sellers (unauthorized Amazon sellers, eBay, gray-market importers) do not carry Kawai factory warranty coverage. We cannot honor warranty claims on instruments we didn't sell.

## What's Covered

- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Electronic component failures under normal use (digital instruments)
- Action irregularities that are not the result of environmental conditions or misuse
- Finish defects present at the time of delivery
- Any failure that occurs under normal playing conditions within the warranty period

---

## What's Not Covered

- Damage from accidents, drops, spills, or misuse
- Damage from environmental conditions: excessive humidity or dryness, flooding, direct sunlight, temperature extremes
- Normal wear: key surfaces, felts, strings (on acoustic instruments tuned and maintained regularly)
- Cosmetic wear from normal use
- Damage incurred during moves arranged by the owner (not by Kawai)
- Instruments that have been serviced, modified, or repaired by unauthorized technicians

## How to File a Warranty Claim

**Step 1 — Contact us**  
Email warranty@kawai.com or call 1-800-XXX-XXXX. Have your order number ready.

**Step 2 — Diagnosis**  
For digital instruments, we may be able to diagnose the issue remotely. For acoustic instruments, we'll schedule a technician visit.

**Step 3 — Resolution**  
Depending on the issue:
- **In-home service**: a certified Kawai technician comes to you (included for eligible models)
- **Parts replacement**: we ship the part and walk you through installation, or arrange technician installation
- **Full replacement**: for instruments with significant defects that cannot be reasonably repaired

We do not charge diagnostic fees for warranty claims.

## Warranty Registration

Your warranty is automatically registered when you purchase from kawai.com using your email address. You'll receive a warranty confirmation email within 24 hours of your order.

No paper cards to mail. No portals to navigate. It's registered the moment you buy.

## Transferability

Kawai warranties are transferable to subsequent owners of the instrument. To transfer, the new owner should contact warranty@kawai.com with proof of the original purchase and their contact information.
