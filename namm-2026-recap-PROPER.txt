<p>The Anaheim Convention Center came alive January 22-24 as Kawai Piano presented an unforgettable lineup at NAMM 2026. Our booth showcased the company's iconic grand piano craftsmanship and digital innovation, with performances from breakout stars and world-class pianists that captivated the music trade show audience. From American Idol sensation Abi Carter to viral TikTok influencer David Snyder, the three-day event highlighted why Kawai remains the choice of professional musicians and emerging artists alike.</p>
