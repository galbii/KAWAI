## Founder: Koichi Kawai

Source: `/company/koichi-kawai/page.tsx`

### A Dream Is Born
Over a century ago in Hamamatsu, Japan, a reed organ builder was struggling alone to build an upright piano from imported parts. One day he saw a neighbor's son riding a unique **pedal-driven cart** — the first ever on the roads of Hamamatsu. The boy had designed and built it himself. The piano builder invited him to be his apprentice. The next day, the invitation was accepted — and the young man, **Koichi Kawai, the son of a wagon maker**, set out to build his first piano.

### Inventive Genius
- Led the R&D team that introduced pianos to Japan
- First in Japan to design and build a **complete piano action**
- Received many patents for designs and inventions

### Birth of the Company (1927)
During the 1920s, the Japanese piano industry began to falter. The company that employed Koichi was struggling, and new management had taken control. Confident that the pursuit of excellence would always bring opportunity, Koichi formed the **Kawai Musical Instrument Research Laboratory** in Hamamatsu in 1927 with seven kindred colleagues. Their shared dream: to one day build the world's finest piano.

### Early Challenges
1. **The Craftsmen Problem** — Shortage of qualified craftsmen meeting Kawai's exacting standards; required heavy investment in training
2. **The Materials Problem** — Scarcity of quality materials in post-war Japan; demanded creative solutions and relentless supplier relationships
3. **The Market Problem** — Underdeveloped dealer network; building distribution from the ground up

By the early 1950s, Kawai had grown to **over 500 people producing 1,500+ pianos per year**.

### The Emperor's Recognition
Koichi Kawai received the prestigious **Blue Ribbon Medal** from the Emperor of Japan — becoming the first person in the musical instrument industry to receive such an honor.

### The Family Legacy (4 generations)
1. **Koichi Kawai** — Founder (1927)
2. **Shigeru Kawai** — Son (president 1955); introduced scientific approach
3. **Hirotaka Kawai** — Grandson (1989); robotics + global expansion
4. **Kentaro Kawai** — Great-grandson; current generation

## Company Philosophy

Source: `/company/our-philosophy/page.tsx`

Embodied in the phrase: **"Crafting Inspiration"** — describes both what they do (physically crafting instruments) and why (inspiring musicians to reach new heights of artistic expression).

### Four Core Beliefs

1. **Craftsmanship Above All**
   Every Kawai piano is the product of skilled craftspeople. From hand-crafting soundboards to regulating each action component, human care is irreplaceable.

2. **Innovation Through Science**
   Never content with "good enough." Investment in research and materials science — from ABS composites (1970) to ABS-Carbon today — reflects belief that scientific progress and musical tradition are not in conflict.

3. **Music for Everyone**
   Quality instruments should be accessible at every level. Product range spans entry digital pianos to the Shigeru Kawai concert grand.

4. **Three-Generation Commitment**
   Family stewardship ensures Kawai's values endure. Koichi's dream is carried forward by Shigeru, Hirotaka, and Kentaro.

### Four Philosophy Pillars (from `/about`)
- **Music is for Everyone** — quality instruments accessible at every level
- **Scientific Innovation** — traditional Japanese craftsmanship + cutting-edge research
- **Crafting Inspiration** — every piano designed to inspire artistic achievement
- **Three-Generation Legacy** — family leadership maintains values across generations

## Architecture Notes

### Three Page Patterns

1. **Static file-based React** (most company content)
   `/about`, `/technology`, `/company/*`, `/the-winners-choice`, `/distinguished-owners`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/return-policy`, `/news`, `/resources`, `/guides`, `/serial-number`, `/find-my-piano`, `/digital-piano-rebate`
   - Hardcoded data; updated via code changes
   - Build-time generation; no DB queries

2. **CMS-driven hubs** (Payload-fetched)
   `/careers` (JobListings), `/faq` (FAQ collection), `/technical-support-division` (SupportGroups), `/warranty-registration` (HubSpot config)

3. **Catch-all `[...slug]`** (Payload Pages collection)
   - Used for general CMS pages, NOT used heavily for company info
   - Company content is intentionally code-controlled

### ISR / Revalidation
- Most pages: `revalidate: 3600` (1 hour)
- `/find-my-piano`: 300s (5 min)
- Drafts and on-demand revalidation via `/api/revalidate`

### Key Design Decision
**Company / brand / historical content lives in code, not the CMS.** Marketing copy for `/about`, `/technology`, `/company/*`, `/the-winners-choice`, `/distinguished-owners` is hardcoded React. Only support/FAQ/jobs/warranty pull from Payload.
